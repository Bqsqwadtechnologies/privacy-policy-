Privacy Policy

Heartbreak Healer — a personal reflection and wellbeing companion. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Effective date: November 25, 2026  ·  Application: Heartbreak Healer

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Heartbreak Healer Android application (“App”), provided by Bq Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us” or “our”). It explains the information involved in using the App, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information you choose to provide

Depending on the features you use, you may enter personal content such as journal entries, unsent letters, memories, reflections, trigger notes, preferences, and other information you choose to save. The App may also process information needed to maintain feature settings, Hearts balances, and your progress.

Your private journal entries, unsent letters, memories, and trigger notes are not used by us to select or personalise advertisements, and are not provided to Google AdMob for ad targeting. Avoid entering information you do not want handled by the App.

3. Advertising, house promotions, and rewarded videos

The App displays Google AdMob advertisements, which may include banner, native, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded formats. We use Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) technologies to serve ads and present consent choices where required. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and consent or privacy choices. The information collected can depend on your device settings, region, consent status, and the ad configuration in use.

The App may also display a clearly labeled “Ad · BQ Sqwad AI” house-promotion video for our sister product. This is our own promotional video, not an ad served by Google. It may be offered directly as an optional partner spotlight or as a fallback when Google AdMob rewarded inventory is unavailable. You must choose to watch it; declining, skipping, or closing it does not prevent normal use of the App. A “Learn more” action is separate and is not required to receive an in-app reward.

Where the screen offers 15 Hearts for watching the BQ Sqwad AI house video, the App credits 15 Hearts only after the video playback completes. Where the screen offers a specific premium feature instead, the named content is unlocked only after full playback completion. The current partner-video feature offers can unlock all four premium soundscapes or the premium deep-breathwork session. The reward or content being offered is shown before playback. Skipping, closing early, playback failure, or a static end card does not grant Hearts or unlock content. Hearts and feature-unlock flags are stored locally on your device.

Google AdMob rewarded videos are optional. A completed eligible Google rewarded video may credit 15 Hearts after the ad provider confirms the reward. Certain optional feature visits cost 1 Heart. Urge SOS, Support, Settings, and the Dashboard remain free. Hearts have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide the App’s features, save or display content you choose to keep, maintain settings and Hearts, respond to support requests, address technical problems, protect the App against abuse, display and measure advertising where permitted, and meet legal obligations.

5. Sharing with service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the App, including Google AdMob and Google Play services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or our legal rights.

6. Data storage, security, and retention

Journal entries, unsent letters, memories, trigger notes, mood check-ins, Hearts balances, feature-unlock flags, and Heart Rebuild Journey progress are stored in private app storage on your device. The App does not operate user accounts or upload this personal content to our servers. Daily reminders and journey notifications are generated locally by Android.

If the App closes unexpectedly, it may save a technical crash report in private app storage. The report can include the time, Android version, device manufacturer and model, thread name, and technical exception stack trace. Heartbreak Healer does not automatically upload this report. It is shared only if you choose to copy and send it to support; you can dismiss and clear it from the app.

Information is otherwise handled and retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable law. Google advertising providers apply their own retention practices. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Choose what personal content you enter and use available in-app controls to manage or delete content where provided.
  • Use Android settings to reset or limit your advertising identifier, where available.
  • Manage advertising consent choices through consent options presented in the App or by Google.
  • Contact us to ask a privacy question or request help with personal information.

We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where it is necessary to resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

8. Children and age-related protections

The App is not directed at children under 13. During onboarding, users must confirm that they are at least 13 years old before continuing. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details below. Advertising and consent settings are subject to applicable age-related and regional requirements.

9. BQ Sqwad AI companion link and third-party services

When you choose the clearly labeled “Learn more” or “Proceed with Companion” action, Android checks on-device whether the single configured BQ Sqwad AI companion package can be launched and opens the installed app or the configured store/web destination. We do not collect or transmit the result of this package-availability check. Once you leave Heartbreak Healer, the BQ Sqwad AI privacy policy and terms apply. We do not transfer your Heartbreak Healer journal entries, letters, memories, mood check-ins, or journey content to BQ Sqwad AI.

The App may also rely on third-party services or link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one. Please review those policies before using their services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
