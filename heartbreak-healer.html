Privacy Policy

Heartbreak Healer — a personal reflection and wellbeing companion. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Effective date: November 13, 2026  ·  Application: Heartbreak Healer

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Heartbreak Healer Android application (“App”), provided by Bq Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us” or “our”). It explains the information involved in using the App, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information you choose to provide

Depending on the features you use, you may enter personal content such as journal entries, unsent letters, memories, reflections, trigger notes, preferences, and other information you choose to save. The App may also process information needed to maintain feature settings, Hearts balances, and your progress.

Your private journal entries, unsent letters, memories, and trigger notes are not used by us to select or personalise advertisements, and are not provided to Google AdMob for ad targeting. Avoid entering information you do not want handled by the App.

3. Advertising and rewarded videos

The App displays advertisements, which may include banner, native, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded formats. We use Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads technologies to serve and measure ads.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and consent or privacy choices. The information collected can depend on your device settings, region, consent status, and the ad configuration in use.

Rewarded videos are optional. When you complete an eligible rewarded video, the App may credit Hearts to your balance. The current Hearts rules include 15 Hearts for a completed rewarded video and a cost of 1 Heart for certain optional feature visits. Urge SOS, Support, Settings, and the Dashboard remain free. Hearts have no cash value.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide the App’s features, save or display content you choose to keep, maintain settings and Hearts, respond to support requests, address technical problems, protect the App against abuse, display and measure advertising where permitted, and meet legal obligations.

5. Sharing with service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the App, including Google AdMob and Google Play services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or our legal rights.

6. Data storage, security, and retention

Information is handled and retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable law. Storage and retention can vary by feature and service provider. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Choose what personal content you enter and use available in-app controls to manage or delete content where provided.
  • Use Android settings to reset or limit your advertising identifier, where available.
  • Manage advertising consent choices through consent options presented in the App or by Google.
  • Contact us to ask a privacy question or request help with personal information.

We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where it is necessary to resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

8. Children and age-related protections

The App is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details below. Advertising and consent settings are subject to applicable age-related and regional requirements.

9. Third-party services and links

The App may rely on third-party services or link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one. Please review those policies before using their services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
