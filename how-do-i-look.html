Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BQ Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the How Do I Look? Android application (the “App”). Please read this Policy carefully. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Who we are

How Do I Look? is provided by BQ Sqwad Technologies. For privacy questions, access requests or concerns, contact us at bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com.

2. What the App does

How Do I Look? is a style and presentation feedback tool. Users may take a photo with the device camera or select an image from the gallery. The App analyses selected images on the device to generate style-related scores, sub-scores and tips. Ratings and preferences may be saved locally so users can review history and progress.

Photo analysis is performed on-device. We do not operate a service that uploads user photos to BQ Sqwad servers for analysis. Photos are not sent to us unless a user independently shares content outside the App (for example through the device share sheet after explicitly choosing to share).

Scores and tips are for entertainment and style inspiration only. They are not medical, psychological or professional advice, and they are not a measure of a person’s worth or attractiveness.

3. Information the App handles

Photos and image content. When a user grants camera or photo access and selects an image, the App processes that image on the device to produce analysis results. Images used for analysis remain under the user’s control on the device. Temporary capture files may be stored in app cache for processing and may be cleared by the system or when app data is removed.

Ratings and local history. The App may store on the device: overall and sub-scores, mode labels, tips text, timestamps, favourite flags, and optional local references to images the user chose to keep with a rating.

App preferences. The App may store local settings such as age confirmation status, notification preference, reduce-motion preference, theme preference and streak-related counters. These records are not a BQ Sqwad online account profile.

Support messages. If a user emails us, we receive the sender address and the content of the message so we can respond and handle follow-up.

Advertising-related information. When advertising services are active, Google and its partners may process limited device and usage information as described in Section 5. That processing is separate from on-device photo analysis.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when the user chooses to capture a photo for analysis. The App does not continuously record video in the background.
  • Photos / media (via system picker where available): used to let the user select an image for analysis. Access is limited to the content the user chooses.
  • Internet: used for advertising, consent tooling and other network features that require connectivity. Internet access does not mean photos are uploaded to our servers for analysis.
  • Notifications: used only if the user enables optional reminders (for example daily style reminders). Notification permission is requested at runtime where required by Android.

Users can manage permissions in Android Settings. Denying a permission may disable the related feature without affecting unrelated parts of the App.

5. Advertising and consent

The App is designed to support Google AdMob advertising. When ads are enabled and available, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interactions, approximate location derived from network data, and ad delivery or measurement data. The exact information depends on the device, region, consent choices and Google’s services.

Where required by law (including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar jurisdictions), consent and privacy choices are presented before personalised advertising is requested, using Google’s User Messaging Platform or equivalent tooling. Users may change available advertising privacy options where the App provides access to those controls.

We do not use the content of user photos for advertising targeting. Google handles information collected through its advertising services under its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. How we use information

Information handled by the App is used to:

  • provide on-device analysis and display results the user requests;
  • maintain local history, favourites and preferences;
  • operate optional reminders when enabled;
  • support advertising and measure ad performance where enabled;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect the security and integrity of the App.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use user photos to train our own advertising models.

7. When information may be shared

  • Google and advertising partners: information processed by AdMob and consent services when those services are active.
  • Service providers: only as needed to operate a feature, host policy pages, or respond to support, subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal and safety matters: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public or our legal rights.
  • User-initiated sharing: if a user chooses to share a result through the device share sheet or another app, the destination service receives what the user sends.

8. Retention and deletion

Local ratings, preferences and cached capture files remain on the device until the user deletes them in the App, clears app storage, or uninstalls the App, subject to Android backup settings. Temporary analysis files in cache may be removed automatically by the system.

Support emails are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, handle follow-up and meet legal requirements. Advertising providers apply their own retention rules.

9. Security

We use security measures appropriate to the App’s design, including on-device processing for photo analysis and Android platform protections. No mobile application or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should keep Android updated and protect device access with a lock screen where possible.

10. Children and age requirements

How Do I Look? is intended for users aged 13 and older. The App asks users to confirm that they meet this minimum age before use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com so we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent from certain processing. Users can:

  • manage Android permissions in system settings;
  • delete individual ratings in History where that feature is available;
  • clear app storage or uninstall the App to remove local data;
  • turn off optional notifications in Settings; and
  • use available advertising privacy controls where provided.

To make a privacy request, email bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

12. International use

The App may be used in different countries. On-device analysis remains on the user’s device. Google advertising and other providers may process information in countries outside the user’s country, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App, our practices or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date on this page and take reasonable steps to bring significant changes to users’ attention.
