Privacy Policy

Application: BQ Sqwad Data Receipt
Last updated: 25 November 2026
Privacy contact: hello@bqsqwadai.com

This Privacy Policy describes how BQ Sqwad Technologies handles information when you use the BQ Sqwad Data Receipt mobile application (the “App”). It explains the information the App processes on your device, the permissions it uses, the role of advertising services, and the choices available to you.

1. About the App and its provider

BQ Sqwad Data Receipt is provided by BQ Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us” or “our”). The App helps you review Android network-usage information, enter mobile data-bundle details, estimate data costs and prepare data receipts that you can share.

2. Information processed on your device

To provide the features you request, the App may process the following information locally on your Android device:

  • Android network-usage statistics, including data transferred by applications and available foreground or background usage information.
  • Names and package details for apps visible through Android's launcher-intent package-visibility query. The current manifest does not request the broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
  • Mobile data-bundle details you enter, such as bundle size, price, validity period and calibration values.
  • App preferences, including country, currency, theme and notification settings.
  • Session network counters used by the optional Live Cost Bubble to display the total device data transferred during an active session.

This information is used to operate the App's requested features and is stored in the App's private local storage. The App does not send these locally processed network-usage statistics, launcher-visible app details, bundle entries or preferences to BQ Sqwad Technologies' servers. An App account is not required.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Usage Access: Allows the App to read Android network-usage statistics, including available per-app mobile and Wi-Fi totals and foreground/background totals, for data-usage estimates and related features. An in-app disclosure explains this access before Android Usage Access settings are opened. If you decline, features that depend on these statistics may show limited estimates.
  • Launcher-app visibility: The manifest uses a scoped launcher-intent query to display apps with launcher activities. The App does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES; visible app details are processed locally.
  • Notifications: Supports reminders and status notifications where you enable them.
  • Display over other apps: Optional permission used only when you enable the Live Cost Bubble.
  • Foreground service: Keeps a Live Cost Bubble session running only after you start that feature. Android displays an ongoing notification while it is active; you can stop the feature in the App or from its notification.
  • Internet and network state: Supports advertising services and opening links that you choose to visit.

The App does not use a VPN, intercept internet traffic, or inspect the contents of websites, messages or traffic belonging to other applications.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertising services are active, Google and its advertising technology providers may process information such as an advertising identifier, device information, diagnostics and ad-interaction information to deliver, measure and protect advertising services. The information processed may depend on your device, region, consent choices and the services enabled.

Where required, a consent form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform is presented before relevant advertising processing. Where Google's consent status requires a privacy-options form, an Ad privacy choices entry appears in the App's Settings so you can reopen that form. Google handles information collected through its services under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Rewarded advertisements may unlock selected App features for a stated period. Watching an advertisement does not provide cash, airtime, mobile data or a physical prize.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell locally stored network-usage statistics, installed-app lists, bundle entries or App preferences. Information may be processed by Google AdMob and related Google services as necessary for advertising delivery, consent management, fraud prevention and measurement. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

When you choose to open a link, the destination website or service may process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Information processed locally by the App remains in the App's private storage until you remove it using an available reset function, clear the App's data through Android Settings or uninstall the App. Clearing local App data does not delete information already processed by Google or another advertising provider. Advertising-related information is handled according to the applicable third-party provider's retention practices and privacy settings.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not designed or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the details below.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can manage Android permissions, clear locally stored App data and, where Google's consent status requires it, open the Ad privacy choices entry in the App's Settings to revisit the UMP privacy-options form.

To submit a privacy enquiry or request, email hello@bqsqwadai.com. We may need to verify a request before taking action.

Applicable data-protection requirements may include the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other privacy laws that apply to your use of the App.

9. International processing

Some third-party service providers, including advertising providers, may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is subject to the providers' terms and any safeguards required by applicable law. The App's locally stored usage information remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, our data practices or applicable legal requirements change. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
